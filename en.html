Who are we?

An association,
a shared passion

Born from a mutual love of singing, our choir brings together voices from all walks of life in joy and good company.

The choir was formed in 2022 and was little more than a karaoke session for a few months, but it quickly established itself and by the end of the year had given 2 performances.

By 2025 that had increased to 11 performances, with 2026 likely to be the same. Our objective is to have fun, and that is reflected not only in the types of concerts we choose to give but also in the number of concerts.

The choir brings together enthusiastic singers in the Évran area. Whether you are an experienced singer or a curious beginner wanting to discover choral singing, you are warmly welcome among us.

The membership of the choir is approximately 50/50 French and English speakers, as is our repertoire, which skilfully blends great English songs — pop, musical theatre, classical — and French songs, creating joyful concerts that have the whole audience singing along.

Rehearsals take place in a relaxed and supportive atmosphere. No need to read music: all you need is the desire to sing and to enjoy yourself! Rehearsal tracks are provided for all parts to aid home practice.
